# Overview

The Overview page is where you start. It shows what is in the vault, what is waiting, and what you can send without a delay. It is the first page under Watch.

## What you see

- **Balance.** Available is what you can send or stake now. Staked is earning rewards, and unstaking takes effect after a delay. Locked stake is part of your stake that the network holds back for now. It cannot be unstaked until it unlocks. The Fund and Send buttons sit here.
- **Fee wallet.** Its address and its balance, and the vault's fee balance when your vault has one. The fee wallet pays gas. The fee balance pays relayed calls. They are different pots. See [Costs](/v1/vigil/costs).
- **Waiting.** How many actions are waiting and how many are ready. Open Waiting to act on them.
- **Protection.** How many devices must approve a change. Open Security to change it.
- **Recent.** The last three things the vault did, newest first. Open History for the rest.
- **Auto-settle line.** It says whether a fee grant pays to settle your waiting actions for you. Off means you press Settle yourself. See [Fee](/v1/vigil/app/fee).

A new vault also shows first steps: fund the vault, add an allowed address, make a first test send.

## Block heights

The live block height is in the sidebar next to the Vigil name. Click any block number in the app to see it as a date and time instead. Click again to switch back. A block takes about 10 seconds.
