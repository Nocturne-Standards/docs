# Seasons

Seasons is an optional programme of quests. You do the quests the normal way in Vigil, count your device keys once, and claim badges after the season ends.

## The season

The page shows the season's state: not open yet, starting soon, running, counting results, claims open, or over. Dates are block numbers, and the times are at least that long.

## Count my vault activity

Counting is optional and is needed once per season. It links the device keys on this computer to the fee wallet. No funds move from your vault, and nothing in it changes. The fee wallet pays the gas for one transaction. The app asks for the folder password first.

## Quests and badges

Quests show Done or Not yet. Badges are claimed after the season ends, once the results are published on chain. The claim window has an end. Badges not claimed by then lapse. The fee wallet receives the badges.

If the season data cannot be read, everything else in Vigil still works.
