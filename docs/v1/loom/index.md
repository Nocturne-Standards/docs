# Loom

**Status:** wip · not audited. Work in progress: a committee publishes a value, such as a NAV mark, for other contracts to read.

Loom is where a named committee writes a value other contracts can read. A net-asset-value mark or a price is the kind of value it is for. The committee signs the value, and the published mark is what a consumer trusts until the committee replaces it.
