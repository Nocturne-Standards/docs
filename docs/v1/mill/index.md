# Mill

**Status:** wip · not audited. Work in progress: a facility splits a credit pool into tranches of different seniority.

Mill is structured credit. A facility holds a pool and splits it into tranches, so one slice takes losses before the next. Who may enter the facility is the same kind of compliance check as [Lien](/v1/lien/): prove on Dusk, then act on DuskEVM.
